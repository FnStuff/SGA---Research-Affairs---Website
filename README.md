# SGA---Research-Affairs---Website
This is the repository for Research Affair's Website.

I will not use any AI during these labs or projects. All questions will be answered by a mentor, professor or a freind. All will be credited. 

The goal of this project is to create a website for IIT students and the public to view what research is being at IIT. Students will also be able to see what labs have availabilities. 
Researchers can update their blogs with new findings and share them with the public on their day to day activities.
